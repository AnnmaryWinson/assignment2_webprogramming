A look at the curriculum

From first concepts to real-world solutions.

Explore a sample four-year learning pathway. The actual course structure may vary according to the official syllabus.

YEAR 01

Building the basics

Get started with programming, computing mathematics, digital logic, communication skills and structured problem-solving.

YEAR 02

Understanding computer science

Explore data structures, algorithm design, object-oriented programming, database systems and computer architecture.

YEAR 03

Exploring systems & emerging fields

Study operating systems, computer networks, software engineering, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and specialised technical subjects.

YEAR 04

Putting knowledge into practice

Bring your skills together through a major project, internship or industry exposure, advanced electives and professional development.

PRACTICE

Learning by doing

Strengthen your understanding through programming tasks, laboratory experiments, collaborative assignments and practical demonstrations.

NEXT STEPS

Preparing for your future

Create a portfolio of your work and explore opportunities in software development, research, specialised technology roles and postgraduate education.
