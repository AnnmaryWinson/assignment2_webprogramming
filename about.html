The B.Tech CSE Department empowers aspiring technologists to tackle real-world challenges through critical thinking, technical expertise and responsible innovation.

Our journey

Every breakthrough starts with a question.

At Federal Institute of Science and Technology, our department combines essential computer science principles with hands-on engineering and a passion for discovery. We encourage students to turn theoretical knowledge into practical skills and approach challenges with confidence.

By offering collaborative learning opportunities, academic mentorship and ongoing skill development, we equip students to adapt to evolving technologies and build rewarding careers.

  • Strong fundamentals for lasting understanding
  • Responsible innovation that values people and diversity
  • Knowledge sharing across different disciplines
  • Growth through reflection and constructive feedback
OUR VISION To nurture forward-thinking professionals who apply computing to create meaningful change.
OUR MISSION Learn with purpose. Innovate with courage. Contribute with care.
